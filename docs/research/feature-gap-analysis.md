# LPG Distribution SaaS — Feature Gap & AI Opportunity Analysis

**Scope:** multi-tenant LPG cylinder distribution platform for the Indian market (PSU-OMC-appointed distributors: Indane / Bharatgas / HP Gas).
**Date of research:** 2026-08-18. **Method:** web research against regulator, ministry, OMC and vendor primary sources.
**Confidence convention:** claims are cited. Where a source is secondary or a policy is known to fluctuate, it is marked `[unverified]` or `[policy-variable]`.

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. The platform is architecturally strong but is currently a **generic delivery/inventory system**; almost none of the *India-specific LPG distributor compliance surface* is modelled.
2. The single largest gap cluster is **OMC Marketing Discipline Guidelines (MDG) 2022 compliance** — the document that decides whether a distributorship is fined or terminated. It prescribes weighment, records, delivery-time star ratings, and irregularity categories the platform cannot currently evidence.
3. **Targeted Delivery Time (TDT) star rating** (quarterly, booking-date → delivery-date, fines of 10–30% of average monthly commission) is measurable from data the platform already holds but is not computed.
4. **Cylinder identity is missing.** The platform tracks *balances by status*, not *individual cylinders*, so statutory-test-due segregation, defective returns and per-cylinder traceability are impossible.
5. **Weighment is missing entirely** — a ±10 g platform digital scale, 10% sample check on receipt and 100% check before load-out are mandatory, and their absence is itself a listed irregularity.
6. **Consumer-lifecycle documents** (SV, TV, DGCC, DBC) and the **security-deposit ledger** tied to them are absent; the platform has "deposit tracking" but not the voucher instruments that govern it.
7. **Subsidy/PAHAL reconciliation and PMUY eligibility validation** are absent — PMUY mis-issuance is a *Critical* irregularity leading to termination.
8. **Compliance-calendar assets** (PESO Form F licence expiring 30 September, scale verification, insurance, biennial customer safety inspection, 5-year Suraksha hose) have no registry.
9. **Cash/UPI reconciliation per delivery staff** with day-close variance is the #1 feature Indian competitors lead with, and the platform has invoices/payments but no daily settlement.
10. Highest-ROI AI work is **classical ML** (refill-cycle prediction, diversion/anomaly detection, credit risk), with **LLMs** best used for conversational ordering in Indian languages, KYC document extraction, complaint triage and an analytics copilot.

## 4. AI integration opportunities

`ML` = classical / statistical modelling. `LLM` = large-language-model-suited. `Hybrid` = LLM front-end over a deterministic or ML back-end.

| # | Feature | User problem | Data the platform already has | ML vs LLM | Approach | Impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | **Refill-cycle prediction & proactive reminders** | Consumers run out and then demand same-day delivery, wrecking TDT and route efficiency | Per-consumer order history, delivery dates, cylinder type, household size proxies, seasonality, branch | **ML** | Per-consumer inter-refill interval model (gradient-boosted survival / hazard model or a simple Bayesian per-customer interval with hierarchical shrinkage for sparse customers). Emit `refill_due_date`; feed the reminder engine and the demand forecast. Guardrail: never nudge inside the mandated booking gap (R14). | **High** | **Low–Med** |
| **A2** | **Branch/day demand forecasting for indent planning** | Under-indenting causes stockouts (Minor irregularity 2.2.7); over-indenting ties up working capital and godown licensed capacity | Historic daily off-take by branch × cylinder type, calendar, festivals, weather, PMUY/commercial mix | **ML** | Hierarchical time series (branch × SKU), gradient boosting with calendar/festival regressors; reconcile bottom-up to tenant level. Output daily indent recommendation vs the 2-day-off-take norm. | **High** | **Medium** |
| **A3** | **Route optimisation & dynamic dispatch** | Manual route building; drivers backtrack; TDT star rating slips | Route stops, GPS breadcrumbs from POD, addresses, vehicle capacity, service times, order priority/age | **ML** (OR, not deep learning) | Capacitated VRP with time windows via OR-Tools; seed clusters with historical actual travel times learned from POD GPS rather than map estimates. Re-optimise on the existing dispatch board over WebSockets. | **High** | **Medium** |
| **A4** | **Diversion / pilferage / leakage anomaly detection** | Domestic cylinders diverted to commercial use; cylinders "delivered" in software but not physically; godown shrinkage | Per-consumer consumption rate, delivery GPS vs registered address, POD timing patterns, stock reconciliation variances, staff-level cash variance, filled/empty splits | **ML** | Layered: (a) rule-based tripwires (consumption > k× peer median for the customer class; GPS-to-address distance; multiple deliveries to one geohash), (b) isolation forest / robust z-scores on staff-day and route-day feature vectors, (c) rank-ordered case queue rather than auto-block. Highly defensible given R10 and D20. | **High** | **Medium** |
| **A5** | **Conversational ordering on WhatsApp / IVR in Indian languages** | Consumers book by phone; showroom lines jam; OMCs are pushing digital booking channels | Customer registry with mobile numbers, order history, price lists, booking-gap rules | **Hybrid (LLM front, deterministic back)** | LLM handles intent + slot-filling in Hindi/regional languages over WhatsApp Business API; **all** state changes go through the existing order API with server-side eligibility, booking-gap and entitlement checks. For voice, Bhashini ASR/TTS (22 languages, government-provided REST/WebSocket APIs) or a commercial Indic ASR. Never let the model quote a price or confirm an order it computed itself. | **High** | **Medium** |
| **A6** | **KYC document AI** | Manual keying of Aadhaar/PAN/address proof is slow and is the direct cause of Major irregularity 2.1.8 (wrong Aadhaar/bank against consumer number) | Uploaded KYC documents in S3, customer records | **Hybrid** | Prefer **structured sources first**: DigiLocker-issued Aadhaar XML / UIDAI offline eKYC XML are digitally signed and machine-readable — parse, do not OCR. Use a vision-LLM only as fallback for legacy scans, always with a human-confirm step and field-level confidence. Note the DigiLocker Aadhaar XML carries a ~1-year "time to live" whereas the UIDAI-downloaded offline XML does not. Mind DPDP (R21) on storage/retention. | **High** | **Medium** |
| **A7** | **Complaint auto-triage & routing** | Complaints arrive as free text/voice in mixed languages; delayed leakage/refill complaints are a **Major** irregularity (2.1.16) | Complaint text, customer history, order state, branch/staff assignment | **LLM** | Classify into category (leakage / delay / overcharge / equipment / staff conduct), severity, and suggested owner; auto-link to the offending order; auto-escalate safety categories to a hard SLA. Small, well-bounded, low-risk LLM task. | **High** | **Low** |
| **A8** | **Photo-based cylinder condition & delivery verification** | Delivery photos are captured but never inspected; dents/cuts must be detected (MDG 1.4(b)) and false PODs deterred | POD photos, GPS, timestamps, cylinder status history | **ML** (vision) | Start with a lightweight classifier: is a cylinder present, is a seal intact, is the image a re-photograph of a screen, does EXIF/GPS agree with the stop. Dent/cut severity grading needs a labelled dataset — treat as phase 2. Advisory only; never auto-reject a delivery. | **Medium** | **Medium** |
| **A9** | **Credit risk scoring for commercial customers** | Commercial 19 kg credit is where bad debt lives; limits are set by gut feel | Invoice/payment history, ageing, order cadence, outstanding empty cylinders, tenure, business category | **ML** | Gradient-boosted classifier on 60/90-day delinquency with a monotonic-constraint set for explainability; output a recommended credit limit band + reason codes. Must be advisory with human override — an opaque credit denial is both a business and a fairness problem. | **High** | **Medium** |
| **A10** | **Churn / attrition prediction (consumer portability)** | Consumers port to another distributor; the distributor loses the annuity and the security deposit relationship | Refill interval drift, complaint history, TDT actuals per customer, non-home-delivery incidence, subsidy failures | **ML** | Survival model on "days since expected refill" plus service-quality features; the actionable output is usually *service recovery*, not discounts. Feed a retention worklist. | **Medium** | **Low–Med** |
| **A11** | **Voice-to-order / voice-to-report for field staff** | Delivery staff and godown keepers cannot type efficiently, especially in local scripts | Driver app audio capture; existing order/stock APIs | **Hybrid** | Indic ASR (Bhashini pipelines support ASR, ASR+NMT, ASR+NMT+TTS) → constrained intent parser → existing API. Confine to a small, closed command vocabulary (record weighment, log defective cylinder, close stop) with visual confirmation before commit. | **Medium** | **Medium** |
| **A12** | **Internal analytics copilot** | Owners and managers cannot self-serve questions like "which route lost us TDT last quarter" | The entire operational schema plus the reporting module | **LLM** | Text-to-SQL over a curated, read-only semantic layer (views, not raw tables), with **tenant_id enforced by RLS at the DB session, never by the generated SQL**. Return the query alongside the answer. Refuse rather than guess on ambiguous metrics. | **Medium** | **Medium** |
| **A13** | **MDG risk copilot** | Owners do not know which MDG clause they are currently exposed on | TDT actuals, weighment records, record-retention state, ineligible-connection candidates, PMUY tags | **Hybrid** | Deterministic rules compute exposure per MDG clause; an LLM writes the plain-language explanation and the remediation checklist. The numbers must never come from the model. Pairs with D8 and is the strongest differentiator on this list. | **High** | **Medium** |
| **A14** | **Delivery-time (ETA) prediction for customers** | "Where is my cylinder" calls consume showroom staff | Route sequence, historical service times, GPS traces | **ML** | Gradient boosting on historical stop-to-stop durations; publish over the existing WebSocket channel to the customer app. | **Medium** | **Low** |

**Deliberately not recommended:** LLM-generated pricing, LLM-computed subsidy or entitlement decisions, and fully autonomous complaint closure. All three are regulated or penalty-bearing surfaces where a plausible-but-wrong answer is worse than no answer.

## 7. Explicit uncertainties

Recorded so that nothing here is mistaken for a settled compliance position.

1. **MDG edition.** §2 is grounded in the **MDG 2022** text (effective 01-May-2022) retrieved from HPCL. IndianOil's index also lists a **"Market Discipline Guidelines-2024"**; that document was not retrieved and may amend thresholds, fines or the TDT bands. **Verify the current edition with the tenant's own OMC before hard-coding any number.**
2. **Statutory retest interval.** Gas Cylinders Rules 2016 Rule 35(1) defers the period to **IS 15975**; the standard itself was not retrieved. The commonly quoted "5 years" in search results referred to **US DOT/49 CFR** requalification, not to Indian LPG cylinders. **Do not hard-code an interval** — model it as configurable reference data sourced from IS 15975.
3. **Refill booking gap (25/45 days)** rests on news reporting of a MoPNG measure taken amid supply constraints, not on a retrieved notification. Treat as tenant-configurable policy.
4. **Biennial customer safety inspection.** The IOCL service-charge schedule confirms a chargeable *"Mandatory Inspection of domestic installation"*; the **two-year cycle** is from press reporting, not a retrieved circular.
5. **Legal Metrology permissible weight error** for a 14.2 kg cylinder was not retrieved as a numeric value. MDG references the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 "as amended from time to time" — model as reference data, do not invent a tolerance.
6. **Weighing-instrument reverification period.** A secondary source indicates 24 months for several instrument classes; the applicable period for a platform digital scale at an LPG godown was not confirmed from a Legal Metrology department source. MDG only requires that the scale be in working condition **with a valid certificate** — build to certificate-expiry, not to an assumed interval.
7. **DPDP phased-compliance sub-deadlines.** PIB confirms the Rules were notified 14-Nov-2025 with an **eighteen-month** phased period. The per-rule commencement schedule was not extracted; consult the gazetted Rules.
8. **OMC distributor-portal APIs.** No public evidence of a documented integration API for distributor software was found. Assume portal-based/manual reconciliation and design an import/export boundary rather than a live integration.
9. **DBC** in Indian LPG means **Double Bottle Connection**, not a delivery-staff commission. Any earlier requirement note using it in the latter sense should be corrected.

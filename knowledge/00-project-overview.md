# Project Overview

## Project Name

LPG Agency Management Platform

---

# Vision

Build a modern, enterprise-grade, AI-first LPG Agency Management Platform that digitizes the complete LPG cylinder lifecycle for distributors.

The platform should replace disconnected spreadsheets, desktop software, and manual processes with a scalable cloud-native SaaS platform.

The system is designed for long-term maintainability, extensibility, accessibility, and operational efficiency.

---

# Project Goals

The platform should provide an end-to-end solution for LPG distributors covering:

- Customer Management
- Cylinder Booking
- Delivery Management
- Inventory Management
- Cylinder Ledger
- Accounting
- Payments
- Reporting
- Notifications
- Administration

Future phases will include:

- AI Demand Forecasting
- Route Optimization
- WhatsApp Booking
- QR/Barcode Tracking
- Government LPG Integration
- Customer Analytics

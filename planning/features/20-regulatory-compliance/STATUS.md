## Exception: Weighment — 🟢 DONE

A second item, this time `PLAN.md`'s own explicit Tier-0 priority ("cheap effort, high consequence" — mirrored in `docs/research/feature-gap-analysis.md` §5), was built as its own narrower slice: MDG 2022 cl. 1.2 (goods-receipt scale + 10% sample) and cl. 1.4(c)(d) (100% load-out check). Regulatory numbers were researched, not invented: ≤10g scale least count and the 10%/100% sample sizes trace to MDG 2022 cl. 1.2/1.4, already cited in `docs/research/feature-gap-analysis.md` R1; the 150g domestic-cylinder (14.2kg) tolerance is a **new finding**, not previously in this repo's own research (medium confidence — multiple independent sources agree it derives from the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, but no exact rule-number citation was pinned down), so it is seeded as a tenant-configurable `weighment_tolerance_grams` default, not a hard constant. MDG editions are per-OMC, not one national standard — this repo's citations are HPCL's 2022 edition; a tenant on a different OMC/edition (e.g. IOCL's 2024 edition) should confirm its own thresholds before relying on the seeded defaults. Gates re-run and green, not assumed:
